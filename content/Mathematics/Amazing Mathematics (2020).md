#### Question 1
Simplify the algebraic expressions below.
	(a) $3x + 2x$
	(b) $2x - 6x$
	(c) $-x + 3x$
	(d) $-2x - 4x$

<details>
<summary>Solutions</summary>

a) $3x + 2x$

$$
\begin{align*}
3x + 2x &= 5x \\
\end{align*}
$$

(b) $2x - 6x$

$$
\begin{align*}
2x - 6x &= -4x \\
\end{align*}
$$

(c) $-x + 3x$
$$
\begin{align*}
-x + 3x &= 2x \\
\end{align*}
$$

(d) $-2x - 4x$
$$
\begin{align*}
-2x - 4x &= -6x \\
\end{align*}
$$

#### Question 2
Find the sum of two or more of the algebraic expressions below.
	(a) $2y + 4, 5y - 1$
	(b) $-4y - x, 3y + x$
	(c) $x - 3y - 1, -y - 3, 2x + 5$
	(d) $5x - 4y, x + 2y - 3, -5x + y$
	(e) $-x + 2y -7, 5y - 3x, 6y + 4, 2 + x$

<details>
<summary>Solutions</summary>

(a) $2y + 4, 5y - 1$
$$
\begin{align*}
2y + 4, 5y - 1 &= 2y + 4 + (5y - 1) \\
&= 2y + 4 + 5y - 1 \\
&= 2y + 5y + 4 - 1 \\
&= 7y + 3 \\
\end{align*}
$$

(b) $-4y - x, 3y + x$
$$
\begin{align*}
-4y - x, 3y + x &= -4y - x + (3y + x) \\
&= -4y - x + 3y + x \\
&= -4y + 3y - x + x \\
&= -y \\
\end{align*}
$$

</details>
