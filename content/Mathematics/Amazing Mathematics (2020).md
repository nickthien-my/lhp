1. Simplify the algebraic expressions below.
	(a) $3x + 2x$
	(b) $2x - 6x$
	(c) $-x + 3x$
	≤(d) $-2x - 4x$
	
2. Find the sum of two or more of the algebraic expressions below.
	(a) $2y + 4, 5y - 1$
	(b) $-4y - x, 3y + x$
	(c) $x - 3y - 1, -y - 3, 2x + 5$
	
### Solutions
1. (a) $3x + 2x$

$$
\begin{align*}
3x + 2x \\
5x \\
\end{align*}
$$

1. (b) $2x - 6x$

$$
\begin{align*}
2x - 6x \\
-4x \\
\end{align*}
$$

2. $4x^2 - 16x$
$$
\begin{align*}
4x^2 - 16x \\
4x(x-4) \\
\end{align*}
$$
