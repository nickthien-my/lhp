# Practice 6.1 - Question 4

Solve the following linear equations by using the trial and improvement method.
a) $3 + 3r = 15$

> [!hint]- Answer (Click to reveal)
> $r =4$

> [!success]- Working (Click to reveal)
> $$
> \begin{align*}
> \text{Let }r = 1 &: \\
> 3 + 3(1) &= 3 + 3 \\
> &= 6 (smaller) \\
> \text{Let }r = 2 &: \\
> 3 + 3(2) &= 3 + 6 \\
> &= 9 (smaller) \\
> \text{Let }r = 3 &: \\
> 3 + 3(3) &= 3 + 9 \\
> &= 12 (smaller) \\
> \text{Let }r = 4 &: \\
> 3 + 3(4) &= 3 + 12 \\
> &= 15 \\
> \text{Hence, }r &= 4 \text{ is the solution to the linear equation.}
> \end{align*}
> $$
